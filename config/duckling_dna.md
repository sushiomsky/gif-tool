# Duckling DNA

Use this as the immutable character and style contract for every generated
duckling image. The request-specific idea is appended after this document.

## Purpose

Create consistent, humorous DuckDice meme illustrations in the established
animated DuckDice comic-GIF style. The duckling is worried but cute: anxious,
nervous, or softly panicked in an endearing way.

## Character and style

- Always show an anthropomorphic yellow duckling (the DuckDice duckling) with oversized
  expressive eyes, orange beak and orange webbed feet. Keep the face,
  proportions, and recognizable duckling identity consistent.
- Use the established comic look from the GIFs: thick dark ink contours,
  saturated yellow/blue/orange colors, readable cel shading, and a polished
  illustrated background. This is comic art, not a flat icon or an isolated
  sticker cutout.
- Blue hoodies, jackets, caps, casino clothing, dice, coins, laptops, casino
  tables, neon rooms, and dramatic comic props are welcome when relevant.
- Compose a square meme frame with a clear foreground character and a simple
  but finished comic scene. Keep the joke readable at small GIF size.
- Captions are short, bold, meme-like words or phrases such as OMG, NO WAY,
  BRUH, SAD QUACKS, STONKS, or THE DUCKLING. Put them in a speech bubble or a
  yellow banner with clean, legible lettering when a caption is requested.
- Do not use manga or anime style.
- Do not make the result photorealistic, generic stock-cartoon, soft pastel,
  flat vector-only, isolated sticker-only, anime, or an unrelated 3D mascot.
  Do not redesign the DuckDice duckling.
- Follow the attached reference images when reference images are supplied.

## Composition

- Produce one single comedic illustration per request.
- Use the requested pose, emotion, and props.
- Favor anxious visual details such as sweat, hiding, nervous eyes, or tense
  posture.
- If a caption is requested, put only a short meme-like caption inside the
  image on a yellow scroll banner.

## Response contract

- Prioritize image generation.
- Do not add long explanations, dialogue, roleplay, or unrelated text.
